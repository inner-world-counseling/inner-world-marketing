Insurance and Payment

Investment and Insurance

Starting therapy is a significant commitment, and you deserve complete transparency regarding your care. I believe you should never be caught off guard by the costs of your sessions. I am committed to being honest and clear about fees before we begin our work together.

No Surprises Act

You are entitled to a full understanding of your financial investment. I provide clear information upfront so there are no unexpected costs. While I strive for total accuracy, I always encourage you to contact your insurance carrier to verify your specific coverage and benefits details.

Your Right to a Good Faith Estimate

You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining how much your care will cost. Under the law, health care providers need to give clients who don't have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the expected charges for services. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency services, upon scheduling a service, or on request. If you receive a bill that is at least $400 more than your Good Faith Estimate, you can dispute the bill. Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate. For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit www.cms.gov/nosurprises or call 1-800-985-3059.

Private Pay Rates

If you prefer to pay out of pocket, or if I am not in network with your specific plan, self-pay rates are shared on request and confirmed in your Good Faith Estimate before your first session.

Out of Network Coverage

If I am not in network with your insurance provider, you may still be able to receive reimbursement through your out of network benefits. Upon request, I can provide a Superbill. This is an itemized document containing the clinical information, such as diagnosis and procedure codes, required by insurance companies. You can submit this document directly to your insurer to seek potential reimbursement.

Pro Tip: I recommend calling the member services line on the back of your insurance card and asking: "Do I have out of network benefits for outpatient mental health, and if so, what is my reimbursement rate for a superbill?"

Payment and Cancellation Policy

I accept most major credit and debit cards. Your payment method must be securely stored in the client portal before your scheduled appointment.

Please note that appointments canceled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours notice are subject to a late cancellation fee. This policy also applies to no show appointments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. I am currently in network with several major providers, and the panels differ by state. See the Accepted Insurance Plans list above for the full list covering North Dakota, Minnesota, and Ohio. I always encourage you to contact your insurance carrier to verify your specific coverage and benefits details.

Which insurance plans do you accept in North Dakota?

In North Dakota I am in network with Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Dakota, Sanford Health Plan, and UnitedHealthcare/Optum/Medica/UMR.

Which insurance plans do you accept in Minnesota?

In Minnesota I am in network with Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Minnesota and UnitedHealthcare/Optum/Medica/UMR.

What if you are not in network with my plan?

If I am not in network with your insurance provider, you may still be able to receive reimbursement through your out of network benefits. Upon request, I can provide a Superbill. This is an itemized document containing the clinical information, such as diagnosis and procedure codes, required by insurance companies. You can submit this document directly to your insurer to seek potential reimbursement.

Do you accept Medicare or Medicaid?

No. I am not in network with Medicare or Medicaid. Medica, which appears in my UnitedHealthcare/Optum/Medica/UMR listing above, is a separate commercial insurer, not Medicare or Medicaid. If you have Medicare or Medicaid coverage, please contact your plan to ask about your options before scheduling.

Do you prescribe medication?

No. I do not prescribe medication. If you work with a prescriber, such as a primary care provider or psychiatrist, I can coordinate your care with them with your written consent.
