Anxiety Therapy in North Dakota and Minnesota

Deirdre Kuvaas, LPCC, offers online anxiety therapy for adults in North Dakota and Minnesota.

You're tired of overthinking everything, and still feeling like you're getting it wrong.

You replay conversations, wonder whether someone is upset with you, and spend more time anticipating what could go wrong than enjoying what's happening now. Even when things are okay, your mind finds something else to worry about.

People may see you as capable and dependable. They might not see how much energy it takes to keep everything together, or how hard you are on yourself along the way.

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • You second-guess decisions and look to others for reassurance.
  • Saying no leaves you feeling guilty.
  • You feel responsible for keeping everyone happy.
  • You struggle to relax because there is always something you "should" be doing.
  • Small mistakes turn into hours of self-criticism.
  • You feel tense, irritable, or exhausted from being in your head.

You may know your worries aren't giving you the whole picture. Knowing that doesn't always make them quieter.

A different relationship with anxiety

In therapy, we'll get curious about what keeps these patterns going. We may explore the pressure to get everything right, the fear of disappointing people, or experiences that taught you to question yourself.

Together, we'll work on practical ways to respond to worry, tolerate uncertainty, and make room for your own needs. That might mean setting a boundary without spending the rest of the day questioning it, letting a conversation end without mentally reviewing every word, or resting without having to earn it first.

My approach draws from CBT, acceptance-based strategies, and other therapeutic tools, shaped around your needs. When past experiences are part of what keeps anxiety going, we can make space to address those, too.

You can show up as you are

You don't need to organize your thoughts before you come in. There's room for the rambling, the frustration, the humor, and the things you feel like you "should be over."

I offer online therapy for adults in North Dakota and Minnesota, and I'm also licensed in Ohio.

Addressing GAD and Daily Worry

While many people struggle with the underlying trauma that fuels anxiety, some also live with Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD). This often presents as a relentless "what if" cycle, a brain that is constantly scanning the future for danger. If you feel like your mind is running a marathon while you are trying to sleep, you are not just "a worrier"; you are managing a physiological state of alarm.

Practical Tools for Immediate Relief

I do not believe in waiting months to see progress. While we work on healing the roots of your anxiety, I also provide practical, evidence based tools to help you reclaim your daily life:

  • CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy): We will identify the specific patterns and "what if" loops that keep your anxiety high. By breaking these down into smaller, manageable parts, we can disrupt the cycle before it dictates your day.
  • Solution Focused Strategies: We will identify the small, concrete changes you can make this week to build momentum. We focus on what is currently working for you and amplify those strengths, helping you regain a sense of agency and control.
  • Nervous System Regulation: Anxiety is not just a mental exercise. We will use targeted techniques to down regulate your nervous system, teaching your body how to shift from "fight or flight" into a state of rest.

My goal is to provide you with a toolkit that you can use the moment you feel the tension rising. You will walk away from our sessions with more than just a place to vent; you will have a clear, actionable plan to manage your symptoms while we do the deeper work of healing the past.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does anxiety therapy with you look like?

We'll get curious about what keeps your anxiety going, like the pressure to get everything right, the fear of disappointing people, or experiences that taught you to question yourself. Together we'll work on practical ways to respond to worry, tolerate uncertainty and make room for your own needs. My approach draws from CBT, acceptance-based strategies and other tools, shaped around what you need.

Do I need to know what to talk about before I start?

No. You don't need to organize your thoughts before you come in. There's room for the rambling, the frustration, the humor, and the things you feel like you "should be over." We can start with what you're noticing now.

How does EMDR help with physical symptoms of anxiety?

EMDR helps process the underlying trauma and stress that keep your nervous system in a constant state of "fight or flight." By addressing these root causes, EMDR can significantly reduce physical symptoms like muscle tension, racing heart, and sleep disruption.

EMDR:
An evidence-based psychotherapy that enables people to heal from the symptoms and emotional distress that are the result of disturbing life experiences.
CPT:
A specific type of cognitive behavioral therapy that helps patients learn how to modify and challenge unhelpful beliefs related to trauma.
