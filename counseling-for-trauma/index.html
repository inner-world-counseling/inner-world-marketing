Trauma Therapy in North Dakota and Minnesota

Deirdre Kuvaas, LPCC, offers online trauma therapy for adults in North Dakota and Minnesota.

You understand that the past is over. You still feel its effects.

Maybe you find yourself bracing for someone's reaction, shutting down during conflict, or feeling ashamed for having needs. You might question your memories, minimize what happened, or wonder why something from years ago still affects you.

Sometimes you can explain exactly where a pattern comes from, and still feel stuck in it.

You don't have to prove it was “bad enough”

You may be seeking support after a specific event, such as sexual assault or another frightening experience. Or you may be trying to understand the effects of repeated criticism, emotional neglect, or relationships where your needs and boundaries weren't respected.

There is room to talk about what happened and how it affects you, even if you aren't sure what to call it.

You might notice:

  • Feeling on guard or having difficulty relaxing around others.
  • Unwanted memories, upsetting dreams, or strong reactions to reminders.
  • Going quiet, feeling disconnected, or struggling to find words during conflict.
  • Feeling responsible for other people's emotions.
  • Difficulty trusting yourself or maintaining boundaries.
  • Shame or self-blame that persists even when you logically know better.

If these responses have been part of your life for a while, you can learn more about PTSD and how it is treated.

Making sense of the ways you learned to cope

Staying quiet, keeping people happy, or trying to do everything perfectly may have helped you get through difficult experiences. Those responses can also become exhausting or get in the way of the relationships and life you want now.

In therapy, we'll explore these patterns with curiosity and compassion. We'll work toward helping you feel more present, respond to distress, and have more choice in how you relate to yourself and others.

Trauma therapy at your pace

My approach may include EMDR, cognitive approaches, and parts-informed work, depending on your needs and preferences. We'll talk together about what each approach involves and what feels appropriate for you.

We'll make room for preparation, coping skills, and ongoing check-ins about the pace of treatment. You have a say in what we focus on, and you can tell me when something feels like too much.

Our work may include addressing painful memories, loosening self-blame, strengthening boundaries, and building trust in your own feelings and perceptions.

You deserve space for your experience

You don't need a perfectly clear story or the right words to begin. We can start with what you're noticing now.

I offer online trauma therapy for adults in North Dakota and Minnesota, and I'm also licensed in Ohio.

You Do Not Have to Relive the Past to Heal It

The most common fear about therapy is that you will be forced to recount every detail of your history. My approach is different. I focus on the process of reprocessing rather than the necessity of recounting.

We utilize specialized, evidence-based approaches, specifically EMDR and CPT, not just as tools to talk about the past, but as ways to Rewire the Nervous System. These therapies are designed to update how your brain and body store traumatic stress. By shifting the power that past events hold over your present life, we work with the beliefs and physical sensations you carry today, allowing you to heal the roots of your trauma at your own pace. You will never be asked to share more than you feel ready to share.

Frequently Asked Questions About Trauma Therapy

Do I have trauma if my childhood was “fine”?
Yes. Trauma is not limited to overt abuse or catastrophic events. It can result from long-term wear and tear, such as navigating an environment where your needs were rarely the priority, managing others' moods, or silencing your own voice to feel secure.
Can I process trauma without having to relive every detail?
Absolutely. You do not have to recount every painful memory to heal. Specialized therapies like EMDR or CPT focus on the process of reprocessing trauma and rewiring the nervous system, working with the beliefs and physical sensations you carry today.
How do I know if I’m ready for trauma therapy?
You may be ready if your past survival strategies, like perfectionism, hyper-vigilance, or constant people-pleasing, are now causing you exhaustion and burnout. If you are ready to stop just managing symptoms and start healing the source, trauma therapy can help.
EMDR:
An evidence-based psychotherapy that enables people to heal from the symptoms and emotional distress that are the result of disturbing life experiences.
CPT:
A specific type of cognitive behavioral therapy that helps patients learn how to modify and challenge unhelpful beliefs related to trauma.
